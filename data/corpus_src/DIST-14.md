# Circular SYN-XXXX/14/2025: Cyber Resilience Framework for Stock Brokers

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This circular applies to all stock brokers whose average monthly client base exceeds 25,000 unique accounts. Brokers below this threshold shall comply with the controls in Annexure B within nine months of the notification date, being 1 April 2026.

## 2. Incident Reporting

Any cyber incident affecting trading systems, client data or order routing shall be reported to the exchange within 3 hours of detection, followed by a root-cause note within 15 days. Failure to report attracts a monetary penalty of Rs 2,00,000 per instance, with a further Rs 25,000 for each day of continued default.

## 3. Controls and Testing

Brokers shall conduct vulnerability assessments every quarter and penetration testing twice in each financial year through an empanelled auditor. Critical findings must be closed within 30 days. Repeated lapses shall result in withholding of 5% of incentives payable to the designated officer until remediation is certified.

## 4. Indemnity and Vendor Obligations

Brokers using third-party cloud or software vendors shall obtain a contractual indemnity covering losses arising from vendor-originated breaches. The indemnity shall be backed by an escrow deposit equal to 6% of annual contract value, held for 12 months from the date of the incident.

## 5. Effective Date

This circular takes effect from 1 April 2026. Existing vendor contracts shall be amended to conform within 90 days of that date.
