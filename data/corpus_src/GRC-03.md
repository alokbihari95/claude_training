# SEBI Circular SYN-SEBI/BRSR/2025

## 1. Applicability
The Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) is mandatory for the top 1000 listed entities by market capitalisation.

## 2. BRSR Core assurance
- Reasonable assurance on BRSR Core key performance indicators by an independent assurance provider is required for the top 150 listed entities from FY 2025-26.
- Limited assurance applies to the remaining entities.

## 3. Value chain disclosures
The top 250 listed entities shall disclose BRSR Core indicators for their value chain partners covering at least 75% of purchases and sales by value.

## 4. Governance
The Board's Risk Management Committee shall oversee ESG risk, using a recognised framework such as ISO 31000, and map each identified ESG risk to a control owner.

## 5. Penalty
Non-compliance attracts a monetary penalty of up to Rs 25 lakh per filing and may lead to trading restrictions.
