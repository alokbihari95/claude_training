# SYN-HPL/SHA-41/26/2025: Shareholders Agreement Summary, Helix Pharma Ltd

## 1. Drag-Along Right

Where Promoter Group holders intend to transfer shares representing 58% or more of paid-up equity capital, they may require the remaining shareholders to sell pro rata on identical terms. A drag notice shall be issued at least 30 days before the proposed completion date. Any indemnity given by a dragged shareholder is capped at the consideration that shareholder receives.

## 2. Tag-Along Right

Minority shareholders holding not more than 10% may elect to sell their full holding alongside any Promoter transfer that results in a change of control. The election must be communicated in writing within 14 days of the transfer notice. Five percent of the purchase price shall be held in escrow for 90 days against warranty claims.

## 3. Board Reserved Matters

The following require the affirmative vote of the Investor Director in addition to a simple majority of the Board:

(a) issue of equity or convertible instruments exceeding ₹75 crore in any financial year;
(b) related-party transactions above ₹4 crore;
(c) borrowings exceeding 1.5 times net worth;
(d) any regulatory filing involving renewal of a product licence.

## 4. Default and Penalties

Breach of a reserved matter control attracts a penalty of 2% per month on the amount affected, accruing from the date of breach until cure. Persistent non-compliance for two consecutive quarters entitles the Investor to nominate an additional independent director, effective on notice to the Company Secretary dated 1 April 2026.
