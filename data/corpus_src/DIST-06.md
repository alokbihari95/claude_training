# Circular No. SYN-4417/6/2025: Refund of Unutilised Input Tax Credit under Inverted Duty Structure

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This circular applies to registered persons whose input goods or services carry a higher rate of tax than the output supplies, resulting in accumulated unutilised input tax credit. It covers supplies made on or after 1 April 2025 and all refund claims filed under the fictional Notification No. 19/2025-Central Tax.

## 2. Computation of Refund

The refundable amount equals the unutilised credit attributable to inputs, multiplied by the ratio of net turnover of inverted-rated supplies to adjusted total turnover. Claims below Rs 25,000 in a tax period are not admissible.

## 3. Provisional Refund and Withholding

Officers may sanction up to 90 per cent of the claimed amount provisionally. The balance of 10 per cent shall be withheld pending verification, which must conclude within 60 days of filing. Where a discrepancy exceeding 5 per cent is found, the entire provisional amount may be recovered with interest at 18 per cent per annum.

## 4. Indemnity, Escrow and Internal Controls

Claimants seeking provisional refunds above Rs 2 crore must furnish a bank guarantee or deposit an escrow equal to 15 per cent of the excess, and execute an indemnity undertaking covering reversal of wrongful credit. Taxpayers shall maintain documented reconciliation controls and certify monthly ledger tie-outs.

## 5. Penalties and Effective Date

A false or inflated refund claim attracts a penalty of 100 per cent of the tax wrongly refunded, subject to a floor of Rs 10,000. This circular takes effect on 15 July 2025.
