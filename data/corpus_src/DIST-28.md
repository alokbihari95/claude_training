# Advisory Note on Combination Notification Thresholds and Review Timelines, SYN-4417/28/2025

## 1. Asset and Turnover Thresholds
A proposed combination must be notified where the combined assets in India of the parties exceed INR 9,000 million, or their combined turnover in India exceeds INR 27,500 million. A group-level test applies at combined assets of INR 36,000 million or combined turnover of INR 110,000 million. A de minimis exemption is available where the target holds assets in India below INR 3,500 million.

## 2. Filing Window and Standstill
Notice in the prescribed form must be filed within 30 calendar days of signing the definitive agreement or the trigger event, whichever is earlier. Parties must not close the transaction or integrate operations until approval is received.

## 3. Review Timelines
The Authority shall form a prima facie opinion within 30 working days of receipt of a complete filing. Where it calls for further information, the clock stops and restarts on receipt of the response. The outer limit for a final order is 150 days from filing.

## 4. Penalties and Indemnities
Failure to notify attracts a penalty of up to 1 per cent of the turnover or assets of the parties, whichever is higher. Transaction documents commonly provide for an escrow of 5 per cent of the consideration, and an indemnity for regulatory costs capped at 2 per cent of enterprise value.

## 5. Circular Reference
Parties should monitor circular SYN-CCI/12/2025, dated 1 April 2025, for revised filing forms and fee schedules.
