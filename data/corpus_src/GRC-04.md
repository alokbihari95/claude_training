# Meridian Finance Ltd - Risk and Control Register (ISO 31000 aligned)

Meridian Finance Ltd is a non-banking financial company (NBFC) and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Meridian Components Ltd. The register maps each risk to a treatment control and to the regulatory obligation it satisfies.

## R-12 Failure to identify beneficial owners of company customers
- Control **IC-KYC-07**: Beneficial owner identification at onboarding and periodic updation.
- Mapped obligation: Para 16 of Master Direction SYN-RBI/KYC/2025 (beneficial owner threshold 10%) and Para 24 (periodic updation).
- Control owner: Chief Compliance Officer.
- Residual risk: HIGH (control operates on the superseded 25% threshold).

## R-15 Late filing of Suspicious Transaction Reports
- Control **IC-STR-02**: Daily alert triage with 5-working-day internal deadline.
- Mapped obligation: Rule 8 of the PMLA Rules (7 working days to FIU-IND).
- Residual risk: LOW.

## R-21 BRSR assurance gap
- Control **IC-ESG-03**: Annual independent assurance on BRSR Core indicators.
- Mapped obligation: SEBI Circular SYN-SEBI/BRSR/2025.
- Residual risk: MEDIUM.

## Testing
Controls are tested by Internal Audit under SOP-IA-KYC-03.
