# Prevention of Money Laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules - synthetic extract

## Rule 8 - Suspicious Transaction Report (STR)
A reporting entity shall file an STR with the Financial Intelligence Unit-India (FIU-IND) within **7 working days** of arriving at a conclusion that a transaction is suspicious.

## Rule 9 - Client identification and record retention
Client identification shall follow the beneficial-owner requirement in Para 16 of Master Direction SYN-RBI/KYC/2025.
Records of identity and transactions shall be preserved for **5 years** after the business relationship has ended.

## Rule 10 - Cash Transaction Report
Cash transactions above Rs 10 lakh in a month shall be reported to FIU-IND by the 15th of the following month.

## Section 13 - Penalty
For each failure of a reporting entity to comply with these Rules, FIU-IND may impose a monetary penalty of not less than Rs 10,000 and not more than Rs 1,00,000 per failure.
