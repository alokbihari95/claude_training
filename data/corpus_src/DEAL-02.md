# Companies Act 2013 - valuation provisions (synthetic extract)

## Section 247 - Valuation by Registered Valuer
Where a valuation of shares or assets is required, it shall be made by a Registered Valuer (RV) who is independent of the company and the transaction parties.

## Valuation report content
The report shall state:
- the valuation date and the methodology used (discounted cash flow, net asset value, or market multiples) and the reasons for choosing it,
- key assumptions, and
- the fair value per share.

## Validity
A valuation report is valid for **90 days** from the valuation date. A transaction completed after that period requires a fresh or refreshed report.

## Penalty
A Registered Valuer who knowingly makes a false or misleading valuation shall be punishable with a fine of not less than Rs 1 lakh and up to Rs 5 lakh.

## Use under other laws
A valuation report by a Registered Valuer under this Act is accepted as evidence of fair value for pricing of share transfers under FEMA and for establishing that a transfer is a bona fide commercial transaction under foreign contribution rules.
