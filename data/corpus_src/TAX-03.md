# Circular No. SYN-CBIC/203/2025 (dated 3 March 2025)

Issued by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) on distribution of input tax credit on common input services.

## 1. Mandatory ISD registration
With effect from 1 April 2025 (prospective), any registered person that wishes to distribute input tax credit on common input services (for example head-office audit, IT or management services) to its branches or distinct persons must obtain registration as an Input Service Distributor (ISD) under Rule 39A.

## 2. ISD invoice
Credit may be distributed only through an ISD invoice issued by the ISD. Distribution by ordinary invoice, journal entry or internal debit note is not valid.

## 3. Consequences of non-compliance
- Credit distributed without ISD registration or ISD invoice is denied to the recipient unit.
- Recovery of wrongly availed credit with interest at 18% per annum.
- Penalty of Rs 25,000 per instance.

## 4. Interaction with e-invoicing
ISD invoices are subject to the e-invoicing regime. See the ruling of the 57th GST Council for the date from which ISD invoices must carry an Invoice Reference Number (IRN).
