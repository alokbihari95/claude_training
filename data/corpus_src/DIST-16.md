# Internal Audit SOP-IA-P2P-01: Procure-to-Pay Controls Testing, Orion Capital Ltd (SYN-P2P/16/2025)

## 1. Scope and Sample Selection
Testing covers purchase orders raised between 1 April 2025 and 30 September 2025 across Orion Capital Ltd's three plants. Auditors shall select a statistical sample at 95% confidence with a tolerable deviation rate of 3%, subject to a minimum of 40 transactions per control.

## 2. Vendor Master Controls
New vendor creation requires dual approval by Procurement and Finance. Bank account changes must be verified by call-back to a number held on the prior vendor record. Any vendor with a tax deducted at source (TDS) shortfall above ₹2,50,000 in a quarter is escalated to the Audit Committee within 10 working days.

## 3. Three-Way Match and Payment Release
Invoices are released only where the purchase order, goods receipt note and vendor invoice agree within the lower of 2% or ₹5,000. Manual overrides require written sign-off by a Group Financial Controller. Payment runs above ₹25,00,000 require a second signatory and a 48-hour hold before release.

## 4. Exceptions and Reporting
Each exception is rated Low, Medium or High. High exceptions trigger an indemnity claim review against the vendor where the contract permits, and are reported to the Audit Committee by 15 November 2025. Repeat exceptions in two consecutive quarters reduce the business unit's discretionary spend budget by 10%.
