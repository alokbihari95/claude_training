# State Stamp Duty Circular SYN-4417/30/2025: Share Transfer Instruments and Agreements to Sell

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This circular applies to share transfer instruments and agreements to sell executed in the State on or after 1 April 2026. Instruments executed outside the State are chargeable if received within the State within 45 days of execution.

## 2. Duty Rate and Valuation Floor

Share transfer instruments are chargeable at 0.15 per cent of the consideration or the market value of the shares, whichever is higher. Duty on any single instrument shall not fall below ₹250 or exceed ₹1,20,000. Where shares are transferred through a demat account without a separate instrument, the depository participant shall withhold and remit duty on behalf of the transferor within 7 working days.

## 3. Agreements to Sell and Escrow Arrangements

An agreement to sell attracts duty at 0.05 per cent of the agreed consideration. Where part of the consideration is held in escrow, duty is payable on the full amount at the time of execution, and any indemnity or holdback amount must be disclosed in the agreement.

## 4. Impounding and Penalty

An instrument found under-stamped shall be impounded. The deficit duty is recoverable together with a penalty of 2 per cent per month of the deficit, subject to a cap of 100 per cent of the duty payable.

## 5. Effective Date and Compliance Controls

Parties and counsel shall maintain a stamping control register recording the instrument date, duty paid, challan number and escrow details. Non-compliance noticed in any audit under the controls framework will be notified on or before 31 March 2027.
