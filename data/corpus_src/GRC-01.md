# Master Direction SYN-RBI/KYC/2025 - Know Your Customer (KYC)

Issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to all Regulated Entities (REs), including banks and NBFCs. Read with the Prevention of Money Laundering (PMLA) Rules.

## Para 12 - Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
REs shall carry out CDD at account opening, and whenever there is doubt about previously obtained identification data.

## Para 16 - Beneficial Owner (BO)
Where the customer is a company, the RE shall identify the beneficial owner: every natural person who, alone or with others, holds ownership of or entitlement to **10% or more** of the shares or capital, or who exercises control through other means.
The earlier threshold of 25% is withdrawn from 1 July 2025.
Where no natural person is identified, the senior managing official shall be treated as the beneficial owner.

## Para 24 - Periodic updation
KYC records shall be updated at least once every:
- 2 years for high-risk customers,
- 8 years for medium-risk customers,
- 10 years for low-risk customers.

## Para 41 - Penalty
Failure to comply with Para 12, 16 or 24 attracts a monetary penalty of up to Rs 1 crore per contravention on the Regulated Entity, in addition to any action under the PMLA.

## Para 45 - Reporting
Suspicious transactions shall be reported as required under the PMLA Rules to the Financial Intelligence Unit-India (FIU-IND).
