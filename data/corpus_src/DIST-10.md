# Advisory Note SYN-7741/10/2025: GST Rate on Electric-Vehicle Charging Services

## 1. Background
The 56th GST Council, at its fictional sitting of 14 October 2025, considered whether charging services supplied through public electric-vehicle chargers should attract a concessional rate. The Council recommended 12 per cent for registered suppliers, subject to issue of a notification.

## 2. Scope and Classification
The concessional rate covers supply of electricity for charging motor vehicles at public charging stations. Bundled supplies, such as parking or lounge access, are excluded and remain at the standard rate of 18 per cent. Apportionment must follow a reasonable basis recorded in the supplier's books.

## 3. Transition and Input Credit
Prepaid charging vouchers issued before 1 December 2025 may continue to be taxed at the earlier rate until 31 March 2026. Input tax credit on charger installation contracts remains available, but reversal under the prescribed formula applies where the rate differential exceeds 5 per cent of taxable value.

## 4. Compliance Controls
Operators must maintain a station-wise ledger, reconciled monthly. Failure to furnish the reconciliation within 30 days of month-end attracts a late-filing penalty of ₹5,000 per return, capped at 0.25 per cent of aggregate turnover for the period.

## 5. Effective Date
The notification takes effect from 1 December 2025. A clarificatory circular is expected before 15 November 2025, and the Council will review the rate at its 58th sitting.
