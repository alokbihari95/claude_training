# Project Orion: GST and Labour-Law Due Diligence Summary (SYN-7731/25/2025)

## 1. Scope and Basis
This summary covers Orion Textiles Ltd (the Target) for 1 April 2021 to 31 December 2024. Review relied on GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and purchase registers supplied by management on 14 February 2025.

## 2. GST Findings
Input tax credit claimed on invoices not reflected in supplier GSTR-2B for FY 2023-24 totals Rs 1.82 crore. Estimated exposure, including 18% interest, is Rs 0.41 crore. Reverse charge on job-work inward supplies at 5% was under-discharged by Rs 37 lakh in FY 2022-23. The export exemption for fabric lacked valid LUTs for two quarters.

## 3. Labour-Law Findings
Contract labour engaged through two agencies exceeded the 50-worker threshold under the applicable state rules without registration for seven months. The minimum wage shortfall across 212 workers is estimated at Rs 58 lakh. Provident fund and ESI contributions were paid late in 9 of 36 months, attracting interest at 12% per annum on delayed amounts.

## 4. Recommended Protections
We recommend a special indemnity covering the identified GST and labour exposures, a withholding of Rs 2.6 crore from the purchase consideration, and an escrow of Rs 3.0 crore for 18 months from completion. Release is conditional on final assessment orders received on or before 30 June 2026.
