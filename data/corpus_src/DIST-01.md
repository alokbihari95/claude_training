# Notification SYN-NTF/1/2025: Withholding on Royalty Payments to Non-Residents under Section 94Y

## 1. Scope and Applicability
This notification applies to every Indian resident payer who credits or pays royalty to a non-resident payee in a financial year, whether by cash transfer, adjustment against an intercompany account, or release from an escrow arrangement. Payments for the transfer of rights in a copyrighted article are included.

## 2. Rate and Threshold
Tax shall be withheld at 12 per cent of the gross royalty where the aggregate royalty credited to a single non-resident payee during the financial year exceeds Rs 25 lakh. Once the threshold is crossed, withholding applies to the entire royalty amount credited in that year, not merely the excess.

## 3. Deposit and Filing
Tax withheld shall be deposited with the designated government account by the seventh day of the month following the month of deduction. The payer shall furnish the quarterly statement in the prescribed form within 30 days from the end of each quarter.

## 4. Indemnity and Escrow Holdbacks
Where the payee disputes the applicable rate, the payer may hold the disputed portion in an escrow account for up to 90 days pending a lower-deduction certificate. Any indemnity given to the payee for tax not withheld must be disclosed in the payer's audit file.

## 5. Consequences of Default
Failure to deduct attracts simple interest at 1 per cent per month from the date of deduction to the date of deposit. A penalty equal to the amount not deducted may be levied. This notification takes effect from 1 April 2026; payments under contracts executed before 1 October 2025 remain governed by the earlier regime.
