# Project Meridian - Due Diligence Summary (30 November 2025)

Prepared for Axiom Holdings Pte Ltd on the proposed acquisition of Meridian Components Ltd (aggregate annual turnover Rs 340 crore) and its NBFC subsidiary Meridian Finance Ltd.

## Finding 1 - Withholding on intra-group management fees
During FY 2023-24 and FY 2024-25 the Company paid Rs 84 crore of intra-group management service fees to its parent Meridian Global GmbH without withholding tax under Section 94Z. Estimated shortfall tax at the then 5% rate: **Rs 4.2 crore**, plus interest at 1.5% per month. Penalty exposure equals 100% of the tax unless the shortfall is paid on or before 31 March 2026.

## Finding 2 - GST input service distribution
The Company is **not registered as an ISD**. It distributed Rs 1.1 crore of input tax credit on head-office services to its branches by journal entry, without ISD invoices.

## Finding 3 - E-invoicing
E-invoicing is not enabled in the Company's ERP.

## Finding 4 - KYC at Meridian Finance Ltd
At 38 company customers, a beneficial owner holding between 10% and 25% was not identified, because the control still applies the withdrawn 25% threshold. Potential penalty up to Rs 1 crore per contravention (up to Rs 38 crore in aggregate).

## Finding 5 - Meridian Foundation
The Foundation (8% shareholder) is FCRA-registered. No valuation report has yet been obtained for its shares.

## Recommendations
Rely on the SPA tax indemnity (clause 9.4) for Findings 1 and 2; require Sellers to pay the Section 94Z shortfall before 31 March 2026; remediate the KYC control before Closing; obtain a Registered Valuer report dated within 90 days of the transfer.
