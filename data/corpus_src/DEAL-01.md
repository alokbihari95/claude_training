# Share Purchase Agreement (extract)

**Parties.** Axiom Holdings Pte Ltd, a company incorporated in Singapore (the "Buyer"), and the promoters of Meridian Components Ltd (the "Sellers"), together with Meridian Foundation, a charitable trust holding 8% of the shares.
**Target.** Meridian Components Ltd (the "Company") and its subsidiary Meridian Finance Ltd (an NBFC).
**Purchase Price.** Rs 1,200 crore for 100% of the shares. **Expected Closing:** 30 June 2026.

## Clause 9 - Indemnity
- **9.2 General indemnity.** Sellers indemnify the Buyer for breach of warranties, capped at **20% of the Purchase Price (Rs 240 crore)**, subject to a basket (deductible) of 0.5% of the Purchase Price (Rs 6 crore). Claims must be notified within 24 months of Closing.
- **9.4 Tax indemnity.** Sellers shall indemnify the Buyer for all Pre-Closing Taxes, including withholding tax, interest and penalties under Section 94Z of the Income Tax Act and any reversal of GST input tax credit. The tax indemnity is **not subject to the cap or the basket** and survives for **7 years** after Closing.

## Clause 10 - Escrow
**10%** of the Purchase Price (Rs 120 crore) shall be held with Harbor Trust Escrow Services for **18 months** after Closing as security for indemnity claims.

## Clause 11 - Material Adverse Change (MAC)
A MAC means (a) a reduction of more than **15% in consolidated EBITDA** against the last audited financial year, or (b) any regulatory action that **revokes or suspends the licence** of Meridian Finance Ltd. If a MAC occurs before Closing, the Buyer may terminate the Agreement without liability.

## Clause 12 - Conditions Precedent
(a) Valuation report of a Registered Valuer under the Companies Act 2013; (b) compliance with FEMA pricing guidelines and filing of Form FC-TRS; (c) FCRA compliance in respect of Meridian Foundation's shares.
