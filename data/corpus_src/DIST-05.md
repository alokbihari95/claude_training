# Advisory Note SYN-CBIC/88/2025 on Composition Levy Eligibility for Small Manufacturers

## 1. Aggregate Turnover Ceiling

A registered person engaged in the manufacture of goods may opt for the composition levy where aggregate turnover in the preceding financial year does not exceed ₹96,00,000. For taxpayers in designated special-category States, the ceiling is ₹48,00,000. Turnover is computed on a PAN-wide basis for the purposes of this illustration.

## 2. Supply Restrictions

Composition dealers shall not make inter-State outward supplies or supplies through an e-commerce operator that collects tax at source. Supplies of specified edible preparations attract a fixed levy of 2.5 per cent of turnover in the State, effective from 1 April 2025 under Notification SYN-N/12/2025.

## 3. Option Filing and Reversal of Credit

Intimation in Form SYN-CMP/1 must be filed on or before 15 May 2025 for the current year. Where input tax credit has been availed on capital goods, a reversal equal to 10 per cent of the value of such goods is required on the date of opting in. Failure to reverse attracts a penalty of ₹25,000.

## 4. Loss of Eligibility and Liability Escrow

Where turnover exceeds the ceiling during a year, the person ceases to be eligible from the day the threshold is crossed and must pay tax at normal rates on stock held. Interest at 18 per cent per annum applies to delayed payment. Management is advised to maintain monthly turnover controls and an escrow reserve equal to 5 per cent of projected turnover against this contingent liability.
