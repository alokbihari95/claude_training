# Circular SYN-7741/3/2025: Safe-Harbour Margins for IT-Enabled Services

## 1. Scope

This circular applies to eligible assessees providing IT-enabled services to associated enterprises where the aggregate value of such international transactions does not exceed INR 250 crore in the relevant previous year.

## 2. Prescribed Margin

The prescribed safe-harbour margin is 16.25 per cent on operating cost, computed on a cost-plus basis. Where the assessee's operating margin falls below this rate, the variance shall be treated as an adjustment for transfer pricing purposes.

## 3. Option and Filing

An assessee opting into the scheme shall file Form SYN-STP-1 on or before 31 October 2025. The option remains in force for five consecutive assessment years. Withdrawal within the lock-in period attracts a penalty of 2 per cent of the international transaction value.

## 4. Controls and Security

Assessees shall maintain board-approved transfer pricing controls, including a quarterly intercompany reconciliation. Any downward adjustment exposure shall be backed by an indemnity and escrow arrangement with the foreign associate. Failure to maintain such controls shall attract a penalty of INR 5 lakh per default.

## 5. Effective Date

This circular takes effect from 1 April 2025 and supersedes Circular SYN-7702/2/2024 in respect of IT-enabled services only.
