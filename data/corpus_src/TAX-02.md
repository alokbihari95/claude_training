# Circular No. SYN-CBDT-C/07/2025 (dated 20 August 2025)

Issued by the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) to remove doubts on the scope of Section 94Z of the Income Tax Act (synthetic), as amended by Notification SYN-CBDT/14/2025.

## 1. Clarification (retrospective)
It is clarified that "cross-border technical and consultancy services" under Section 94Z always included intra-group management service charges, shared-service recharges and cost-sharing recoveries paid to associated non-resident enterprises.
This is a **retrospective clarification** and has effect from 1 April 2023, the date Section 94Z was introduced. It is not a new levy.

## 2. Consequence
Deductors who did not withhold on intra-group management charges in FY 2023-24 and FY 2024-25 are liable to withhold at the rate then applicable (5%), together with interest at 1.5% per month under Section 94Z(5).

## 3. Penalty relief
Penalty under Section 271Z shall not be levied where the shortfall tax and interest are deposited on or before 31 March 2026.
After that date penalty equal to 100% of the tax not withheld applies.

## 4. Distinction from the Notification
Notification SYN-CBDT/14/2025 (10% rate, Rs 50 lakh threshold) operates prospectively from 1 April 2025. This Circular clarifies scope only; it does not change the rate for earlier years.
