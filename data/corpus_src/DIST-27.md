# Advisory Note SYN-TKO/27/2025: Open Offer Triggers and Pricing for Listed Targets

## 1. Trigger Threshold
An acquirer crossing a fictional aggregate voting share threshold of 31 per cent of the target's equity capital, whether directly or through persons acting in concert, must make a public announcement within 2 working days. Creeping acquisitions of up to 2 per cent in any financial year are excluded from the trigger, subject to the controls in Section 4.

## 2. Offer Size and Minimum Price
The offer covers a fictional 26 per cent of expanded voting capital. The offer price is the highest of (a) the negotiated price under the triggering agreement, (b) the 60-trading-day volume-weighted average price, and (c) the highest price paid by the acquirer in the preceding 26 weeks. Where the target's shares are thinly traded, a fictional independent valuation report must accompany the offer, and the escrow deposit must equal 25 per cent of the consideration payable up to the first ₹500 crore.

## 3. Escrow, Withholding and Indemnity
Cash or bank guarantee escrow is placed with a designated escrow bank before the detailed public statement is published. A fictional withholding of 10 per cent of the offer consideration may be retained against unresolved demands arising from the target's pre-offer disclosures, backed by an indemnity from the promoter group capped at ₹75 crore.

## 4. Penalties and Circular Compliance
Failure to make the public announcement attracts a fictional penalty of ₹1 lakh per day of delay, subject to a ceiling of ₹25 crore. Voting rights in excess of the trigger stay suspended until cure. Compliance must be evidenced under Circular SYN-SEBI/OO/09/2025, effective 1 April 2026.
