# Fictional Notification SYN-7315/4/2025: Equalisation Levy on Digital Advertising Services

## 1. Scope and Applicability
This notification applies to non-resident online advertisers and digital intermediaries whose aggregate digital advertising consideration received from Indian users exceeds INR 40 lakh in a financial year. Consideration routed through an escrow account held with a scheduled bank is treated as received on the date of release to the beneficiary.

## 2. Levy Rate and Base
The levy is charged at 4.5 per cent of gross consideration, excluding goods and services tax and disclosed discounts. Where consideration is bundled with non-advertising services, the assessee must apportion it on a reasonable basis and record the apportionment in a valuation schedule.

## 3. Withholding and Deposit
Resident payers shall withhold the levy at source on each payment exceeding INR 2 lakh and deposit it within seven days of the month end. Payers who do not withhold must deposit the amount through the prescribed challan by 15 June 2025 for transactions of the preceding quarter.

## 4. Controls, Indemnity and Penalties
Payers shall maintain internal controls that evidence the levy computation and obtain a written indemnity from any non-resident intermediary against short deduction. Late deposit attracts simple interest at 1 per cent per month. A penalty equal to the unpaid amount applies, subject to a cap of INR 10 lakh.
