# SOP-IA-KYC-03 - KYC Periodic Review Testing

## Purpose
To test the operating effectiveness of control IC-KYC-07 at Meridian Finance Ltd.

## Steps
1. Extract the customer master and filter by risk category.
2. Sample 10% of high-risk customers and 5% of medium-risk customers.
3. Verify that KYC records for high-risk customers were updated within the last 2 years.
4. Verify that the beneficial owner is identified at the 10% threshold.
5. Record exceptions in the audit workpaper.

## Escalation
Any exception must be escalated to the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) within 5 working days. Unresolved exceptions are reported to the Audit Committee at its next meeting.

## Records
Workpapers are retained for 8 years.
