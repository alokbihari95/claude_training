# Circular SYN-7741/8/2025: Customs Valuation of Related-Party Imports

## 1. Scope
This circular applies to imports where buyer and seller are related persons. Where the declared transaction value is accepted under the related-party test, the proper officer shall examine whether the relationship has influenced price.

## 2. Contemporaneous Price Comparison
Importers shall furnish comparable import data for the preceding 90 days. Where the declared value falls more than 12 per cent below the average of contemporaneous unrelated-party prices, the declaration shall be provisionally rejected and a show-cause notice issued within 15 days.

## 3. Security and Escrow
Pending final determination, the importer may furnish a bank guarantee or deposit the duty differential into an escrow account at a notified bank. The security shall equal 110 per cent of the differential duty computed on the enhanced value.

## 4. Controls and Indemnity
Importers with related-party imports exceeding ₹50 crore in a financial year shall maintain a documented pricing control framework, reviewed by an independent auditor by 31 March 2026. Parties shall execute an indemnity covering duty, interest and penalty arising from valuation adjustments.

## 5. Penalties and Effective Date
Knowing under-valuation shall attract a penalty of 100 per cent of the duty short-levied, subject to a floor of ₹2 lakh. This circular takes effect on 1 July 2025 and applies to bills of entry filed on or after that date.
