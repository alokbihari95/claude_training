# Advisory Note SYN-GST/7/2025: Reverse Charge on Residential Rent Paid to Registered Persons

## 1. Scope of the Recommendation
Under the fictional 55th Council recommendation, where a registered person takes a residential dwelling on rent for use by its employees, the recipient shall discharge GST under reverse charge at 12 per cent on rent paid to an unregistered landlord. Rent paid to a registered landlord remains under the forward charge.

## 2. Thresholds and Exclusions
Reverse charge applies only where aggregate monthly rent for all dwellings taken on lease exceeds Rs 40,000. Dwellings let at a monthly rent of Rs 25,000 or below fall outside this circular.

## 3. Compliance Controls
Recipients must maintain a rent register, obtain a written landlord declaration of non-registration, and withhold no amount from the landlord's payment. Tax shall be paid by the 20th of the succeeding month through the prescribed return.

## 4. Penalties and Indemnity
Late self-assessment attracts interest at 18 per cent per annum and a penalty of Rs 10,000 per return period. Recipients should obtain an indemnity from landlords and place an escrow of Rs 2 lakh against disputed liabilities.

## 5. Effective Date
The notification takes effect from 1 April 2026 for leases executed on or after that date. Existing leases continue under prior treatment until renewal.
