# Ruling of the 57th GST Council Meeting

## 1. E-invoicing threshold (prospective)
E-invoicing becomes mandatory for every registered person whose Aggregate Annual Turnover (AATO) exceeds Rs 5 crore, down from Rs 10 crore, with effect from 1 January 2026.

## 2. Invoice Reference Number and QR code
Every e-invoice must carry an Invoice Reference Number (IRN) generated on the Invoice Registration Portal, and a QR code. Invoices without an IRN are not valid for claiming input tax credit by the recipient.

## 3. ISD invoices
ISD invoices issued under Circular SYN-CBIC/203/2025 for distribution of input tax credit must also carry an IRN and QR code from 1 January 2026.

## 4. Reporting window
Taxpayers with AATO of Rs 10 crore and above must report invoices to the portal within 30 days of the invoice date.

## 5. Penalty
Failure to issue an e-invoice attracts a penalty of 100% of the tax due or Rs 10,000, whichever is higher, per invoice.
