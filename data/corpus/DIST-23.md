# SYN-4471/23/2025: Advisory Note on Schemes of Arrangement and Merger Approvals

This note summarises fictional procedural controls for schemes proposed under a notional Section 230 framework. It is intended for internal review only.

## 1. Scope and Meeting Thresholds

The scheme applies to a compromise or arrangement between a company and its members or creditors. Approval requires a majority in number, representing at least 70% in value, of those present and voting at the convened meeting. Each class must be separately convened where rights differ materially.

## 2. Filing and Notice Periods

A petition is filed before the Tribunal with a fee of ₹38,500. Notice to the Regional Director and the Official Liquidator must issue within 14 days of filing. Members and creditors receive at least 21 clear days' notice before the meeting, and the notification to the stock exchange must be lodged by 3 March 2025 under circular No. SYN-MCA/12/2025.

## 3. Withholding, Indemnity and Escrow Controls

Where consideration is payable to dissenting shareholders, 2.5% is placed in escrow for 180 days. A 1.5% withholding applies to deemed transfers. The acquirer's indemnity is capped at 10% of the transaction value, with a survival period of 24 months.

## 4. Penalties and Non-Compliance

Failure to file the scheme within the prescribed window attracts a default penalty of ₹75,000, plus ₹2,000 per day of continuing default, subject to a cap of ₹5 lakh. Directors of the company are liable jointly for the penalty unless they establish that they acted diligently.
