# Notification No. SYN-CBDT/14/2025 (dated 12 February 2025)

The Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Income Tax Department, hereby notifies amendments to Section 94Z of the Income Tax Act (synthetic).

## 1. Rate and threshold (prospective)
Section 94Z requires every person making payment of fees for cross-border technical and consultancy services to a non-resident to withhold tax at source.
- With effect from 1 April 2025, the withholding rate is increased from 5% to 10%.
- The annual aggregate threshold is reduced from Rs 1 crore to Rs 50 lakh per payee per financial year.
- These changes are **prospective** and apply only to payments made on or after 1 April 2025.
- For payments made between 1 April 2023 and 31 March 2025 the earlier rate of 5% and threshold of Rs 1 crore continue to apply.

## 2. Compliance
The deductor shall deposit the tax by the 7th of the following month and file Form 27Z quarterly with the Tax Authority.

## 3. Consequences of default
- Interest under Section 94Z(5) at 1.5% per month on tax not withheld, from the date it was deductible until deposit.
- Penalty under Section 271Z equal to 100% of the tax not withheld.

## 4. Related instruments
The meaning of "cross-border technical and consultancy services" is further clarified in CBDT Circular SYN-CBDT-C/07/2025.
