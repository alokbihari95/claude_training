# SYN-ESG/11/2025: Registration Framework for ESG Rating Providers

## 1. Scope and Eligibility

This circular applies to any entity offering ESG rating services to listed entities or investors in India. An applicant must have a minimum net worth of Rs 8 crore in each of the three preceding financial years and must not have any director convicted of an offence involving moral turpitude.

## 2. Application and Fee

Applications shall be filed electronically in Form ESG-R within 45 days of this notification. A non-refundable processing fee of Rs 2,50,000 applies, and an additional Rs 1,00,000 is payable for each methodology submitted for review.

## 3. Governance and Controls

Each provider shall maintain at least two independent directors, an internal review committee, and an indemnity arrangement covering rating disputes. An escrow deposit of Rs 50 lakh shall be held with a scheduled commercial bank for the duration of registration.

## 4. Penalties and Withholding

Non-compliance with Clause 3 shall attract a monetary penalty of up to Rs 25 lakh per contravention. The Board may withhold renewal of registration for any provider that fails to submit annual audited controls reports by 30 June of each year.

## 5. Effective Date

This circular takes effect on 1 April 2026. Existing providers shall seek registration within six months of that date.
