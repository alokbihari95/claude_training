# FCRA - synthetic extract

## Registration and account
An association or trust may receive foreign contribution only if it holds an FCRA registration, and only into its designated FCRA bank account.

## Foreign source
A foreign company, including a company incorporated outside India such as a Singapore entity, is a foreign source.

## Commercial consideration exception
Money received from a foreign source as **bona fide commercial consideration** (for example the price for shares sold) is **not** foreign contribution, provided that:
1. the price is supported by a valuation report of a **Registered Valuer** under the Companies Act 2013, which must be valid on the date of transfer, and
2. the transaction is reported to the authorities within 30 days.
Where these conditions are not met, the receipt is treated as foreign contribution and requires prior permission, failing which the registration may be cancelled.

## Utilisation
Foreign contribution may be used only for the purposes stated in the registration, and administrative expenses are capped at 20%.
