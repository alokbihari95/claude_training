# SYN-OITS/09/2025: Fictional Master Direction on Outsourcing of IT Services by Regulated Entities

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This Direction applies to every regulated entity whose outsourced IT arrangements exceed a fictional annual contract value of ₹4 crore. Arrangements below that value remain subject to board-approved internal controls but need not follow the notification steps in Section 2.

## 2. Material Outsourcing Notification

A regulated entity shall notify the supervisor within 21 days of entering into, renewing, or materially modifying a material IT outsourcing arrangement. The notification must include a risk assessment, a named exit plan, and a certificate from the chief information security officer confirming the service provider's compliance with this circular.

## 3. Controls, Audit and Escrow

Service providers shall maintain a recovery time objective of no more than 4 hours for core banking workloads. Regulated entities shall conduct on-site audits at least once every 12 months and shall deposit source code and configuration escrow equal to 10 per cent of the contract value with an approved custodian. Sub-outsourcing requires prior written consent.

## 4. Indemnity, Penalties and Effective Date

Service agreements shall include an indemnity covering losses from data breaches up to 150 per cent of annual fees. Non-compliance attracts a monetary penalty of ₹25 lakh per instance, with a further penalty of ₹2 lakh per day of continuing default after the notification deadline. This Direction takes effect on 1 April 2026, and existing arrangements must be brought into compliance by 31 March 2027.
