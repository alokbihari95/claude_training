# Guidelines on Board Committees and Corporate Governance for Insurers, SYN-GOV/18/2025

## 1. Constitution of Board Committees

Every insurer shall constitute an Audit Committee, a Risk Management Committee and a Policyholder Protection Committee within 45 days of the date of this notification. Each committee shall have a minimum of three directors, of whom at least two shall be independent directors.

## 2. Composition and Meetings

The chairperson of the Audit Committee shall not be the Chairperson of the Board. The Risk Management Committee shall meet at least once every 90 days. Quorum shall be two members or one-third of the committee's strength, whichever is higher.

## 3. Indemnity and Escrow Controls

Where a related-party reinsurance arrangement exceeds 7.5 per cent of net written premium, the insurer shall place 12 per cent of the consideration in escrow pending committee review. Indemnity cover for committee members shall not exceed two times annual director remuneration. Any claim withholding above Rs 25 lakh requires prior written approval from the committee.

## 4. Reporting and Penalties

Committee minutes shall be filed with the Authority within 15 working days of each meeting. A delay beyond 30 days attracts a penalty of Rs 2 lakh per default, together with Rs 10,000 per day of continuing non-compliance.

## 5. Transition

Insurers in existence on 1 April 2025 shall file a compliance certificate, signed by the Chief Compliance Officer, by 30 September 2025.
