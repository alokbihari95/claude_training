# Orion Capital Ltd: Internal Financial Controls Register, SYN-IFC/19/2025

## 1. Scope and Board Responsibility

This register maps Orion Capital Ltd's internal financial controls to the directors' responsibility statement under Section 134(5)(e) and the audit committee duties under Section 177. The board attests annually, with the report due on 15 July 2025 for FY 2024-25. Each control owner certifies quarterly, and the first certification cycle closes on 30 September 2025.

## 2. Withholding and Tax Deduction Controls

Tax withholding on vendor payments is validated against a rate table revised by circular on 1 April 2025. Payments exceeding INR 2,50,000 in a single month require dual sign-off. Any variance above 0.5 per cent between the computed and deducted amount is escalated to the audit committee within five working days.

## 3. Escrow and Indemnity Controls

Escrow balances for the Kolkata portfolio are reconciled monthly by the finance team. Indemnity claims above INR 10,00,000 are reported to the board within 14 days of notice. Unreconciled escrow differences exceeding INR 1,00,000 for more than 45 days attract a control deficiency rating.

## 4. Penalty Exposure and Remediation

Non-compliance with the reporting requirements may attract a penalty of up to INR 25,000 on the company and INR 10,000 on each responsible officer, subject to the applicable provisions. Remediation plans for material weaknesses must be approved by 31 October 2025, with a follow-up review by the internal auditor on 31 January 2026.
