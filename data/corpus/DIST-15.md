# Risk Appetite Statement under ISO 31000: Orion Insurance Ltd (SYN-ORN-RAS/15/2025)

## 1. Scope and Approval
This statement applies to all lines of business of Orion Insurance Ltd. It was approved by the Board Risk Committee on 14 March 2025 and takes effect from 1 April 2025. It supersedes internal circular OIL-RC/08/2024 and is to be read with the enterprise risk management policy.

## 2. Underwriting Loss Tolerance
The combined ratio for the motor and health portfolios shall not exceed 104 per cent in any quarter. If a line breaches 101 per cent for two consecutive quarters, the Chief Underwriting Officer shall apply a rate revision of not less than 6 per cent and withhold new agency commissions above 12 per cent pending review.

## 3. Capital and Solvency Buffer
Solvency coverage shall be maintained at not less than 185 per cent of the required margin. A reading between 175 and 185 per cent triggers an escrow top-up of ₹40 crore into the designated reserve account within 15 working days.

## 4. Reinsurance and Counterparty Exposure
Aggregate exposure to any single reinsurer shall not exceed 18 per cent of ceded premium. Indemnity recoveries outstanding beyond 180 days attract a provision of 50 per cent. Any reinsurer downgraded below A- shall be placed on a watch list within 10 days of the downgrade.

## 5. Control Breach Escalation
A failure of a key control lasting more than 30 days is reported to the Board within 5 working days. Repeated breaches in one financial year attract a penalty allocation of 0.5 per cent of the business unit's gross written premium, held until remediation is signed off.
