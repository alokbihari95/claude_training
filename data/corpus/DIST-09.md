# Advisory Note SYN-CBDT-P/31/2025: Presumptive Taxation Scheme for Small Professionals

## 1. Eligibility

A resident individual or firm engaged in notified professional services may opt into the scheme if gross receipts in the prior financial year do not exceed ₹85 lakh, and cash receipts do not exceed 5 per cent of total receipts. Opt-in is made through the prescribed circular form before the due date of the return.

## 2. Presumptive Income

Deemed income is computed at 48 per cent of gross receipts for eligible professionals. Taxpayers may declare a higher figure only where supported by books of account audited under the notification's prescribed format.

## 3. Withholding and Advance Tax

Payers deducting tax at source on professional fees shall apply a rate of 10 per cent where the payee has furnished a valid scheme declaration. Advance tax is payable in instalments on 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March, with interest at 1 per cent per month on shortfall.

## 4. Penalties and Indemnity

Failure to file the opt-in form within 30 days of the prescribed date attracts a late fee of ₹5,000. Misreporting of receipts exceeding 10 per cent of declared amounts attracts a penalty of 100 per cent of the tax sought to be evaded. Professionals must indemnify the payer against any shortfall arising from an invalid declaration.

## 5. Effective Date

The scheme applies from assessment year 2026-27. Opt-outs may be exercised within 60 days of the start of any financial year.
