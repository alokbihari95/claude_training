# SYN-RBI-CR/17/2025: Credit Information Reporting by Lenders to Credit Information Companies

## 1. Scope and Reporting Entities

This circular applies to all regulated lenders holding aggregate exposure of ₹5 crore or more with any borrower. Lenders must furnish account-level data to every credit information company (CIC) with which they have an active membership agreement.

## 2. Submission Frequency and Data Standards

Lenders shall submit data monthly, on or before the 7th day of the succeeding month. Each record must carry a unique account identifier, a days-past-due field accurate to the nearest day, and the outstanding principal. Records with suit-filed or written-off status must be reported within 3 working days of the event.

## 3. Correction Window and Borrower Disputes

Where a borrower disputes an entry, the lender shall verify and respond within 21 calendar days. Corrections must be transmitted to all CICs within 2 working days of verification. Lenders retain indemnity obligations for losses caused by erroneous submissions.

## 4. Penalties for Non-Compliance

A failure to submit by the due date attracts a penalty of ₹10,000 per day of delay, subject to a cap of ₹5 lakh per reporting cycle. Repeated defaults in any two consecutive quarters may result in escrow of supervisory fees pending remediation.

## 5. Effective Date and Transition

This circular takes effect on 1 April 2026. Lenders must submit a controls attestation, certified by the board audit committee, by 30 June 2026.
