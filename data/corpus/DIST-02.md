# SYN-CBDT-AR/3/2025: Section 94X Advance Ruling Application Procedure and Timelines

## 1. Eligibility and Application Fee

A non-resident applicant may seek an advance ruling under Section 94X where the proposed transaction involves a withholding obligation exceeding Rs 2 crore in aggregate consideration. The application fee is Rs 25,000, payable through challan before filing. Applications filed without proof of payment are treated as not validly made.

## 2. Form, Declarations and Filing Window

Applications must be made in Form SYN-AR-1 within 90 days of the date on which the transaction was first proposed in writing. The form must be verified by an authorised signatory and accompanied by a statement of the withholding position, a draft indemnity undertaking, and a description of any escrow arrangement proposed for the transaction.

## 3. Admission and Ruling Timelines

The Authority shall admit or reject an application within 45 days of receipt. Where admitted, the ruling is to be pronounced within 180 days. Adjournments at the applicant's request are limited to two, each not exceeding 15 days.

## 4. Non-compliance and Penalty

Incomplete applications are returned once, with a 30-day window to cure defects. Failure to cure attracts rejection without refund of the fee. Withdrawal after admission, without the Authority's consent, attracts a penalty of 2% of the transaction value, subject to a cap of Rs 5 lakh.
