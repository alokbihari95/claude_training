# Share Purchase Agreement Summary: Kestrel Capital and Orion Textiles Ltd (SYN-SPA/21/2025)

## 1. Parties and Consideration

Kestrel Capital (the "Purchaser") will acquire 61.5% of the equity shares of Orion Textiles Ltd (the "Company") for an aggregate consideration of INR 240 crore, subject to the adjustments below. Completion is targeted for 30 September 2025. Tax may be withheld at source on consideration payable to non-resident Sellers.

## 2. Indemnity and Cap

The Sellers indemnify the Purchaser against losses arising from breach of warranty, undisclosed liabilities and pre-completion tax demands. Aggregate liability is capped at 15% of the Purchase Consideration. Individual claims below INR 25 lakh are disregarded, and claim notices must be served within 18 months of completion.

## 3. Escrow

12% of the Purchase Consideration, being INR 28.8 crore, is deposited with an escrow agent for 24 months from completion. Half is released at month 12, provided no claims are pending. The balance is released at month 24, less amounts reserved for open claims.

## 4. Working-Capital Adjustment

Completion accounts are prepared within 60 days. If closing net working capital deviates from the agreed target of INR 46 crore by more than INR 1 crore, the price is adjusted pro rata, subject to a collar of INR 2 crore. Disputes go to an independent chartered accountant, whose determination is final absent manifest error.

## 5. Controls Undertaking

The Company maintains internal financial controls consistent with the Companies Act, 2013. An external compliance review must be delivered by 31 December 2025. Late delivery attracts a penalty of INR 10 lakh per month, payable to the Purchaser.
