# Indicative Term Sheet SYN-4417/29/2025: Minority Investment in Zephyr Logistics Private Limited

## 1. Subscription and Stake
The Investor shall subscribe to 18,50,000 Series B Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares at Rs 412 per share, aggregating Rs 76.22 crore, for an indicative stake of 14.5 per cent on a fully diluted basis. Tranche one of Rs 40 crore is payable within 21 days of signing, targeted for 15 July 2025.

## 2. Liquidation Preference
On a liquidation event, Series B holders receive 1.25 times the subscription amount, non-participating, ahead of Series A and ordinary equity. A sale of more than 50 per cent of voting rights is a deemed liquidation event, and the preference is payable within 90 days.

## 3. Anti-Dilution
If the Company issues equity below Rs 412 per share before 31 March 2027, the conversion price resets on a weighted-average basis, subject to a floor of Rs 330 per share. Issuances under the approved ESOP pool of 6 per cent are excluded.

## 4. Escrow, Withholding and Indemnity
Rs 8 crore of tranche one shall be held in an escrow account with a scheduled bank until the Company delivers a board circular confirming the controls in Schedule C. Tax withholding shall be deducted at the applicable rate and evidenced by certificate. The Founders indemnify the Investor for breach of warranties up to 10 per cent of the subscription amount, with a 24-month survival period and a 2 per cent monthly penalty for delayed notification.
