# SYN-DL/11/2025: Key Fact Statement and Cooling-off Framework for Digital Lending

## 1. Scope and Effective Date
This fictional circular applies to regulated entities and lending service providers offering digital loans through mobile applications, with effect from 1 April 2026. Existing arrangements shall be aligned within 90 days of that date.

## 2. Key Fact Statement
Every borrower shall receive a key fact statement before disbursement. The statement must disclose the all-inclusive annual percentage rate, a processing fee not exceeding 2.5 per cent of the principal, the default penalty rate, and the grievance contact. Acknowledgement shall be recorded through a click-through confirmation.

## 3. Cooling-off Period
A borrower may exit a digital loan without penalty within 3 working days of disbursement, provided the principal and proportionate interest are repaid within that window. No prepayment charge shall apply. Any refund due shall not be withheld beyond 2 working days of receipt.

## 4. Penalties and Controls
Failure to issue the statement or honour the cooling-off right attracts a penalty of up to Rs 5 lakh per instance. Repeated non-compliance within a financial year may lead to restriction on fresh digital lending. Lenders shall maintain an escrow reserve equal to 1 per cent of the outstanding digital loan book, held with a scheduled commercial bank, as an indemnity against borrower refunds.
