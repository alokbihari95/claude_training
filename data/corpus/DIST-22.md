# Advisory Note SYN-RPT/41/2025: Approvals for Related Party Transactions under Section 188

## 1. Scope and Trigger Thresholds

This note covers contracts or arrangements with related parties that fall within Section 188 of the fictional Companies Act, 2013 framework. Under the illustrative model, a transaction exceeding 10 percent of turnover, or 20 percent of net worth, requires a special resolution of members, with the interested director and related parties abstaining from voting.

## 2. Board and Audit Committee Approval

All related party transactions require prior approval of the Audit Committee. Where the Committee's approval is sought after execution, a ratification window of 60 days applies. Board minutes must record the basis of arm's length pricing, supported by a benchmarking study dated within 90 days of approval.

## 3. Penalties and Withholding of Payments

Non-compliance attracts a penalty of ₹5,00,000 on the company and ₹1,00,000 on each responsible officer, with a further daily default fee of ₹10,000. Payments due under an unapproved contract may be withheld pending regularisation, and an escrow of 15 percent of the contract value is advised where regularisation is uncertain.

## 4. Indemnity and Internal Controls

Companies should adopt a written related party policy, circulated by the Company Secretary by 31 March 2026, and obtain an indemnity from interested directors for losses arising from undisclosed arrangements. Quarterly controls testing should confirm that every related party register entry carries a documented approval reference.
