# Circular SYN-7421/13/2025: Related Party Transaction Approval Thresholds under LODR

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This circular applies to listed entities whose equity shares are traded on a recognised stock exchange. It governs related party transactions (RPTs) entered into by the entity or any of its subsidiaries, including transactions involving transfer of resources, services, or obligations, and any indemnity, guarantee, or escrow arrangement with a related party.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

An RPT exceeding the lower of 2.5 per cent of annual consolidated turnover or INR 1,200 crore requires prior approval of the audit committee and a special resolution of the shareholders. Transactions below this threshold require audit committee approval only. Transactions with a value below INR 10 crore, or below 0.5 per cent of turnover, are exempt from shareholder approval but must be reported quarterly.

## 3. Interested Party Controls

A related party, or any director with a material interest, shall abstain from voting on the relevant resolution. Listed entities shall maintain documented controls, including a withholding of payment pending approval where the transaction has not been cleared, and a fortnightly reconciliation of RPT ledgers.

## 4. Disclosure and Penalties

Entities shall disclose approved RPTs to the exchanges within 2 working days of the approval date. Non-compliance attracts a penalty of INR 25 lakh per instance, with an additional INR 50,000 per day of delay, capped at INR 1 crore per financial year.

## 5. Effective Date

This circular takes effect on 1 April 2026 for transactions entered into on or after that date. Existing arrangements shall be brought into compliance by 30 September 2026.
