# FEMA NDI Rules - synthetic extract

## Pricing
A transfer of shares of an Indian company from a resident to a non-resident shall not be at a price less than the fair value determined under an internationally accepted pricing methodology. The fair value shall be certified by a Chartered Accountant, a SEBI-registered merchant banker or a Registered Valuer.

## Deferred consideration and escrow
Up to **25% of the total consideration** may be deferred or held in escrow for a period not exceeding **18 months** from the date of the transfer agreement.

## Reporting
The resident seller or Indian company shall file **Form FC-TRS** with the authorised dealer bank within **60 days** of receipt of the consideration or transfer of shares, whichever is earlier.
Delay attracts compounding under the Foreign Exchange Management Act.

## Sectoral condition
Where the Indian company has a regulated financial subsidiary (such as an NBFC), prior approval of the sectoral regulator may be required for change in control.
